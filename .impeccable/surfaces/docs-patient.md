# Patient pages

Scope: all eight pages of `docs/patient` in English and German (`docs/patient/de`). Visitor mode: Read. Guided path, read in order like chapters.

Audience: friends the author sends a link to, on a phone; people with type 1 who want to understand their body and pump. Job: understand why a setting change moves the glucose curve. Content stays as written; figures replace Mermaid diagrams where a curve says it better. No papers, formulas or tool talk. Not medical advice, said on the first page and in the footer.

Data: `docs/patient/assets/figures.json`, generated by `cargo run -p tir-tuner-sim --example patient_figures`. Every figure is virtual patient output and is labelled so.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the pages are set like the one-page glucose report clinics print. The report's own furniture carries the illness explainer, so a patient recognises it and a friend learns to read it on page one. Refuses the health-explainer default: white cards, line icons, one blue, stock reassurance.

OWN-WORLD: near-black header strip (#1d1d1b) with chapter "n of 8" and the page name in tracked caps; directly under it the stacked time in range bar in the report's five colours (very low #8b1a1a, low #e53935, in range #4caf50, high #f9d648, very high #f28c28), drawn from the virtual patient's data. Figures are ruled report panels: 1px ink rule, panel title in small caps, the 70-180 band tinted pale green, median curve heavy ink, the spread of days as a grey band. White ground, Public Sans throughout, tabular figures. Colour never alone: every band carries its label, lows also a pattern.

STORY: the reader meets the report on page one, learns its bar and its band, then every chapter reuses them, so by the end the reader can read their own clinic report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: phone, 390 wide. Header strip, range bar, language switch, title at 32px heavy, opening paragraph, then the page's first report panel at full column width. Previous and next at the end of the page.

FORM: clinic glucose report (AGP-style printout), the pick card, second on the ordered list; seed fe1236a2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Raises kept: one glucose scale on every glucose figure; every figure has its numbers in a small table under it; the chapter strip marks the chapter you stopped at.

Open: units, mg/dL primary with mmol/L in small type (no switch).
