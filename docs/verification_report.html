CamAPS FX controller core: verification suite catalog

What the formal verification suite checks and which tool checks it: Kani proofs, native property tests, and coverage-guided fuzzing, each tied to the source paper or the patent it implements. Generated from the Rust sources by tools/gen_verification_report.py. This document describes the current state of the suite, not a test run; it contains no pass/fail results.

1. Scope and tool split

Three tools share the workload, chosen by what each is good at. Kani proves universal statements over bounded symbolic inputs whose operations are comparisons, clamps, additions and multiplications by constants. Native Rust tests (exhaustive lattice sweeps, proptest randomized cases) cover the rounding-level claims: sum-to-one identities, dense sweeps, the wired behaviour of the full model. Coverage-guided fuzzing soaks the actual numerical code paths. The split is documented in the blueprint and repeated in the module docs the report is extracted from.

tir-tuner-aps

The controller side of Auto mode, aligned with what Cambridge published: the model of US8977504B2, the NMPC of Hovorka 2004, Boost and Ease-off as Ware 2022/2022b describe them. CamAPS FX itself isn't published, so this is the published lineage, not the product. * [hovorka]: the patent's glucoregulatory model. * [controller]: eq 9 NMPC, the Auto mode variants, the hypo cutoff. * [kalman]: the patent's IMM filter, eq 53-82. * [imm]: its mode-probability arithmetic, eq 66-67 and 80. * [AutoMode]: personal target and weight in the controller's units. Kani proofs live in [verification] (cargo kani).

tir-tuner-common

Shared primitives for the tir-tuner workspace. Everything here is algorithm-independent: numeric integration primitives, unit conversions, clinical reporting metrics and the domain types for recorded therapy data. The [tir_tuner_aps] crate (the verified pump algorithm) and the tir-tuner-body / tir-tuner-cgm simulation crates all build on it without sharing semantics. For a patient-facing companion page, see docs/patient/math-and-metrics.md.

tir-tuner-body

The virtual patient of Wilinska et al. 2010, with Fiasp absorption and the YpsoPump's bolus speed. Eleven compartments: subcutaneous insulin s1/s2, plasma insulin i, insulin actions x1-x3, glucose masses q1/q2, gut g1/g2 and interstitial glucose c. EGP follows Hovorka 2004 eq 1 where W10 has a sign typo. Every number traces to docs/sources.toml. The crate doesn't depend on the controller; the loop lives in tir-tuner-cli. Patient-facing page: docs/patient/body.md.

tir-tuner-cgm

CGM sensor model: interstitial glucose to a whole-mg/dL reading. The error is Breton & Kovatchev 2008 eq 7: an AR(1) driver per 15-min sample, mapped through a Johnson SU transform to a non-Gaussian error in mg/dL. B08 fitted it on the FreeStyle Navigator. No source in the folder models Libre 3 error, so this is the closest published one. For a patient-facing companion page, see docs/patient/sensor.md.

tir-tuner-aps

Kani proofs (cargo kani -p tir-tuner-aps). Kani proves what it handles well: bounded symbolic inputs through comparisons, clamps and short arithmetic. Long f64 chains, the full horizon and the IMM rounding identities are covered by the proptests in hovorka.rs, controller.rs, imm.rs and the fuzz/ targets.

tir-tuner-common

Kani formal verification suite for the shared primitives (only built under cargo kani). # Source clamped_forward_euler's non-negativity is an algebraic fact about IEEE max, not a literature claim; no reference key applies.

tir-tuner-body

Kani proofs for the body model (only built under cargo kani). Bounded symbolic inputs; the transcendental-free submodels and the wiring of the clamped Euler step. The full-state walk is the proptest in solver.rs.

tir-tuner-cgm

Kani proofs for the sensor (only built under cargo kani). The Johnson transform's sinh is out of CBMC's reach; its statistics are native tests in device.rs.

2. What the model is and where it comes from

The crate implements the published lineage of the Cambridge hybrid closed-loop algorithm (CamAPS FX itself isn't published): the glucoregulatory model of patent US8977504B2, subcutaneous insulin and gut absorption submodels, endogenous glucose production (EGP), the interstitial (CGM) compartment, an NMPC-style dose calculator with Ease-off / Boost modes, and interacting-multiple-model (IMM) mode-probability bookkeeping.

      tir-tuner-aps/src/kalman.rs

      Glucose estimation: the patent's interacting multiple model filter (US8977504B2 eq 53-82) on its subset state [q1, q2, q3, u_s] (eq 46, without the bioavailability F). The modes differ only in σ_w, the SD of the change in unexplained glucose influx (eq 65): a quiet mode smooths sensor noise, a loud one follows an unannounced meal. The patent takes the measurement noise as white (eq 62). The simulated sensor's error is B08's AR(1), and a white-noise filter reads its runs as glucose changes, so ours carries the error as a fifth state with B08's coefficient and SD. The patent publishes the structure and no values: the σ_w per mode and the transition matrix are ours.

      7. Out of scope and future work

      The crate runs the patent's IMM filter with three modes and a CGM error state. Not implemented: the estimation of bioavailability and the Bayesian adaptation of the controller's parameters. Marquardt minimization is replaced by a grid seed plus coordinate descent. Every number in the controller is traced in docs/sources.toml, where our own choices have status assumption.
